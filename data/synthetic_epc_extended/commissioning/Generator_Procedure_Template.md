## Page 1

These ordered steps are fictional and exist to exercise deterministic pass and fail evaluation. They are not an external commissioning standard.

### Step 1

**Prerequisite:** Fuel delivery complete

**Instruction:** Verify fuel system integrity and prime the day tank.

**Acceptance criterion:** No visible leakage after 30 minutes at operating pressure.

### Step 2

**Prerequisite:** Load bank connected

**Instruction:** Perform a black-start and record time to accept full load.

**Acceptance criterion:** Full load accepted within 10 seconds.

### Step 3

**Prerequisite:** Black-start passed

**Instruction:** Run a four-hour load-bank test at 100 percent rated load.

**Acceptance criterion:** Coolant temperature stable and within vendor limits for four hours.

### Step 4

**Prerequisite:** Load-bank test complete

**Instruction:** Measure sound level at 7 metres on four sides at full load.

**Acceptance criterion:** All four readings not greater than 75 dBA.

### Step 5

**Prerequisite:** BMS-A integration complete

**Instruction:** Verify alarm annunciation at the EPMS for low fuel and high coolant temperature.

**Acceptance criterion:** Both alarms received and logged at the EPMS.
