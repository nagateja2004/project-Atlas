### Step 1

**Prerequisite:** Delivery and placement complete

**Instruction:** Record insulation resistance on primary and secondary windings.

**Acceptance criterion:** Insulation resistance not less than the vendor minimum at 20 degrees Celsius.

### Step 2

**Prerequisite:** Insulation test complete

**Instruction:** Verify turns ratio on all tap positions.

**Acceptance criterion:** Turns ratio within plus or minus 0.5 percent of nameplate on every tap.

### Step 3

**Prerequisite:** Ratio test complete

**Instruction:** Verify winding temperature monitor alarm and trip set points.

**Acceptance criterion:** Alarm and trip operate at the scheduled set points.

### Step 4

**Prerequisite:** Protection settings applied

**Instruction:** Energize and record no-load current and audible noise.

**Acceptance criterion:** No-load current within vendor limits and no abnormal noise.
