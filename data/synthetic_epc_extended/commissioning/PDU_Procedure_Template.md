## Page 1

These ordered steps are fictional and exist to exercise deterministic pass and fail evaluation. They are not an external commissioning standard.

### Step 1

**Prerequisite:** Upstream energization complete

**Instruction:** Verify primary and secondary voltages against nameplate before load.

**Acceptance criterion:** Measured voltages within plus or minus 2 percent of nameplate.

### Step 2

**Prerequisite:** Voltage verification complete

**Instruction:** Perform insulation resistance testing on the integral transformer.

**Acceptance criterion:** Insulation resistance not less than the vendor minimum.

### Step 3

**Prerequisite:** Insulation test passed

**Instruction:** Verify branch-circuit monitoring against a reference meter on six circuits.

**Acceptance criterion:** All six readings within plus or minus 2 percent of reference.

### Step 4

**Prerequisite:** Monitoring verified

**Instruction:** Confirm the circuit directory matches the as-built distribution schedule.

**Acceptance criterion:** Directory matches the schedule with no unlabelled breakers.
